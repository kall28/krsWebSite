Case Study

Renepay

Background and Objective

Renepay, was developed a recharge module for Renepay, which is Bangladesh’s first online services commerce platform. It allows users to recharge credit in their mobile phones and get value coupons which can be utilized at their favorite restaurants, cafes, beauty parlors and electric stores.

Renepay has also created a strong digital presence, led by its website - http://www.renepay.in

The objective was to attract more visitors, who were interested in a purchase, to the website.
