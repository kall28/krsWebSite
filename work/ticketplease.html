Case Study

Ticketplease

Background and Objective

Ticketplease, The online booking site covers ticketing needs for movies, concerts, plays and sports events..

The website will also enable fans to connect with their favourite celebrities through online chats, blogs, videos and photographs. TicketPlease.com also plans to offer film reviews, features, celebrity news and interviews.

Ticketplease has also created a strong digital presence, led by its website - http://www.ticketplease.com

The objective was to attract more visitors, who were interested in a purchase, to the website.
