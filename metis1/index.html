ABOUT MAHALLATOMAN
Even when on the move, the free Mahallat mobile application for iOS and Android, allows you to order food and other products online anytime from anywhere. Whichever food/ product you currently desire, we have the variety selection of restaurants/shops for you to choose from, right here on Mahallat! In three words, the process of ordering via online is simple, fast, and convenient.
On the homepage you can start your order by entering your location, which lists out the shops or restaurants in your location.
Next, you can select your favorite shop or restaurant you want from the list Now look through the available online menu or products list, when you find something you like, tap to add it to your online basket.
When you are ready to check out, you’ll see your address along the price of the order including tax and delivery fee, when everything looks right, just tap Place order—and that’s it.
Your order will be processed, prepared, and delivered straight to your home, office, or your current location.

How can I order products from Mahallat?

  • Open the Mahallat App and Enter your location
  • Find a shop/ restaurant. Browse the variety of shops and restaurants that deliver to your location. It can be your home, office, or a hotel!
  • Choose your dishes. Browse the menu or products of the chosen restaurant/shops, select your dishes/ products, and add them to your basket.
  • When you are done, press the "Checkout" button.
  • Checkout & Payment. Check your order, payment method selection and exact delivery address. Simply follow the checkout instructions from there.
  • Delivery. We will send you an email and notification confirming your order.
  • Sit back, relax and wait for your food/products to be conveniently delivered to you!
  • Can you pay cash for Mahallat?
  • Yes, you can pay cash on delivery for Mahallat.
  • How much does Mahallat charge for delivery?
  • You can always check the delivery fee while forming your order.
